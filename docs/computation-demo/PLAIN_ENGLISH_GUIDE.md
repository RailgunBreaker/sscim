# SSCIM in plain English

Chips are made through a long chain. A problem at one point—such as a material restriction, equipment outage, earthquake, or capacity decision—can affect later parts of the chain.

SSCIM is a map plus a set of explicit rules for exploring that possibility. It does not say “this will happen.” It says “given this event, this snapshot, and these stated assumptions, these are the parts of the model that become more exposed.”

Use it to learn the structure, compare possible pathways, and decide what to research next. Do not use it as proof of a company outcome or as investment advice.
