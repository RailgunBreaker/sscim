# SSCIM model roadmap

SSCIM is transparent about what it does not yet know. The current model is useful for structured comparison, not calibrated forecasting.

## Highest-value data improvements

1. **Facility and capacity data:** geocoded facilities, process capability, utilization, and expansion plans.
2. **Dependency data:** buyer-input dependence, qualification relationships, bill-of-material evidence, and alternative suppliers.
3. **Operational buffers:** inventory days, lead times, switching time, recovery time, and contract constraints.
4. **Better market denominators:** consistent definitions for foundry, memory, packaging, and chip-product shares.
5. **Outcome dataset:** dated disruption episodes paired with observed operational, price, volume, or capacity outcomes.

## What calibration would require

Calibration needs a held-out event/outcome dataset, a documented target variable, a pre-registered or versioned fitting procedure, and out-of-sample evaluation. Until then, propagation coefficients remain declared priors and model results remain sensitivity outputs.

## Research discipline

Add evidence tiers and provenance to every important input. Preserve historical snapshots. Compare alternative graph and parameter assumptions. Report failure cases as carefully as appealing results.
