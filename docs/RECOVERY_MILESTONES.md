# Site and production-step recovery

The [canonical specification](MODEL_V7_SPEC.md) remains authoritative. This compatible extension changes persistence for an explicitly scoped part of an incident. It preserves the global 60-day outage recovery duration, the 45-day market half-life, incident grouping and propagation equations.

`profile.recoveryComponents` partitions each directly sourced stage's existing exposure. Each component names an `id`, `stage`, `site`, `processStage`, `exposureFraction`, `exposureBasis`, optional assumed `profile`, and `milestones`. Fractions must sum to at most one per stage. Duplicated components and invalid or excessive fractions are reported and leave that stage's original profile in place; they are never normalized into invented measurements. Any uncovered fraction retains the original assumed trajectory. A component cannot create exposure at a stage the incident does not directly source.

The production steps are `restart`, `wafer_input`, `finished_output` and `shipments`. A restart date without a quantified residual documents an occurrence and does not assert capacity restoration. An observation for one step cannot override another step. A numerical milestone must contain an explicit `residualDisruption` in [0,1], `claimStatus: verified`, a supporting URL and section, and valid effective, publication and information-available dates. A source URL alone does not qualify a milestone. Quantitative residuals must describe the defined component; they are not global stage capacity estimates.

For stage exposure alpha, source magnitude is severity intensity times alpha times the sum of each component fraction times its persistence, plus the uncovered fraction times the parent profile. Components remain parts of one jointly propagated incident. Recovery records stay attached as updates and never inject independent mitigating shocks. Source details expose `persistenceByStage`, `recoveryComponents`, and their observed-versus-assumed status; the compatibility scalar `persistence` is an exposure-weighted summary only.

A milestone becomes usable only after both its effective date and the later of its publication and information-available dates. The latest eligible observation overrides that component's curve. Its documented residual is held until a later eligible observation; carrying it forward is an explicit assumption, not a forecast or an interpolated recovery path. Missing dates, missing residuals, unverified claims and absent evaluation dates retain labeled assumptions. Retrospective replay does not apply later publications to earlier dates. Evidence reviewed later can identify a source's earlier publication date, but that alone does not establish a complete historical information vintage.

The verified [Renesas final update](https://www.renesas.com/en/about/newsroom/update-5-final-impact-2026-kumamoto-earthquake-renesas-operations), published August 24, 2026, reports an August 4 production restart and restoration of Kawashiri wafer-input capacity on the evening of August 23. Source verification was performed September 6, 2026, by Codex source review; it is not recorded as a human approval. Its second body paragraph supports the wafer-input observation. It does not support a finished-chip output recovery date, cleared shipments, or earthquake-wide restoration.

The corrected factual `h2607_kumamoto` model therefore describes Kawashiri wafer input only, mapped to the analog/MCU stage. The retained 0.45 coefficient is an assumed dimensionless exposure intensity, not a measured capacity share. The whole of that narrowed source is the wafer-input component, so no new site-share estimate is introduced. It is zero from August 24 on the strength of the scoped observation. This does not assert that the whole earthquake or customer backlog had recovered. The broader original narrative is preserved in the evidence audit trail as unresolved context. Other-site, finished-output and shipment coverage is unknown; unknown coverage is not zero real-world disruption.

Before the observation becomes available, the original linear curve remains an assumption. The historical factual baseline also depends on the separate occurrence-evidence gate. If the only verified occurrence source is the August 24 retrospective statement, earlier factual dates exclude the event until that source becomes available, even though isolated profile tests can examine the earlier assumed curve. The two decisions must not be confused.

`app/src/engine/recoveryMilestones.test.js` tests partial and complete restoration, staggered sites, separate production steps, missing information, publication-date behavior, future incidents, residual persistence beyond the original horizon, and incident deduplication.
